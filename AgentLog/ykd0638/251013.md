**Layer 2: AI Brain (StateTree)**
```
States:
- Wander (배회)
- Approach (다가가기)
- Greet (인사)

- GoHome (귀가 - 시간대별)
```

**Layer 3: Movement (DetourCrowdAIController)**
```
- RVO (Reciprocal Velocity Obstacle) 기반 충돌 회피

```

**Layer 4: Data Driven (UNPCTypeData)**
```
DataAsset으로 타입별 차이 관리:
- WalkSpeed

- ChatDuration
- DaySchedule / NightSchedule
```
